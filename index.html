If your home is getting warm or your system won’t come on, the quickest way to reach us is by phone. A few details help us understand the problem before anyone looks at the system.

We take emergency AC calls 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

  1. 01

    Call us

    Call (407) 456-1329. If you can’t talk right now, send a service request and also call when you can.

  2. 02

Common AC problems

Signs it’s time to call a technician

Notice one of these? Tell us when you call. It helps us understand what’s happening.

  • The house won’t cool down

    The system runs but rooms stay warm, or the air from the vents isn’t cold.

  • Weak airflow

    Little air coming from the vents, or some rooms getting much less than others.

  • Unusual noises

    Grinding, banging, buzzing or squealing from the indoor or outdoor unit.

  • Ice on the coil or lines

    Frost or ice on the indoor coil or copper lines can point to a frozen evaporator coil.

  • Possible refrigerant leak

    Hissing near the lines, ice build-up, or cooling that slowly gets weaker over time.

  • Clogged or dirty air filter

    A filter packed with dust restricts airflow and makes the whole system work harder.

  • Outdoor unit won’t start

    The fan or compressor outside won’t run, or shuts off and on again and again.

This list helps you describe what you’re seeing. It isn’t a diagnosis. Only a technician looking at your system can find the actual cause.
